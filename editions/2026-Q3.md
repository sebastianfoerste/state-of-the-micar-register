# The State of the MiCAR Register - 2026 Q3

MiCAR requires crypto-asset white papers to be made available in a machine-readable format. Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2984 specifies XHTML with Inline XBRL and applies from 23 December 2025.

This edition does not yet test whether registered documents satisfy those requirements. It asks a narrower and reproducible question: what can be observed from the links recorded in ESMA's public register?

## What the numbers say

The register is highly concentrated by home Member State. Ireland, Malta, Germany, the Netherlands, Liechtenstein, and Luxembourg account for most registered white papers in the 7 July 2026 snapshot.

The link data are operationally important but incomplete. A landing page or bare-domain URL requires another retrieval step before its document format can be known. A PDF-shaped or XHTML-shaped URL is also only a candidate classification until the response is fetched and inspected.

The current 12.0% figure therefore describes structured-link candidates. It is not the share of white papers proven to be machine-readable.

## What the numbers do not say

This edition does not establish:

- whether a link resolves successfully;
- the final URL after redirects;
- the response MIME type or file signature;
- whether an XHTML document contains valid Inline XBRL;
- whether a PDF-shaped URL actually serves a PDF;
- whether any white paper complies with MiCAR;
- whether any named issuer omitted a required disclosure.

## Next measurement step

The next edition should fetch each public white-paper link and record HTTP status, final URL, content type, byte length, SHA-256 digest, detected format, parseability, and Inline XBRL markers. Inaccessible content must remain distinct from non-conforming content.

Until that content-level audit exists, the publication gate permits register-link observations only.

## How this was measured

Every figure comes from the [MiCAR Register Observatory](https://github.com/sebastianfoerste/micar-register-observatory), which snapshots ESMA's public register exports and commits changes. This edition is generated from the 7 July 2026 snapshot.

The existing `format_class` values are URL-shape classifications. The analyzer exposes that basis as `register_link_shape` and reports content-verification counts separately.

See [METHODOLOGY.md](../METHODOLOGY.md) for definitions, legal framing, limitations, and the content-verification design.

## Legal boundary

This report states facts about a dated public-register snapshot. It makes no legal assessment of an issuer or white paper and is not legal advice. ESMA states that registered white papers have not been reviewed or approved by a competent authority.
